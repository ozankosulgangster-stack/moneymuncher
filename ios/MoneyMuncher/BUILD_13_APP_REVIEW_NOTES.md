# Money Muncher 1.1 (Build 13) — App Review Notes

## Paste-ready notes

Hello App Review,

Money Muncher 1.1 (build 13) introduces the native **Family Quest Loop**, a family financial-literacy experience using virtual practice coins only.

### Family Quest review path

1. Launch Money Muncher and tap **Family Quest** on the native home screen.
2. The initial **Kid Quest Board** is available without signing in.
3. Tap **Grown-up Camp**. On a clean installation, complete the one-time multiplication check and create a six-digit Parent PIN.
4. Tap **Create a family quest**, enter a short real-world mission and virtual coin reward, then save it.
5. Return to **Kid Quest Board**, find the mission, and tap **I did it!**
6. Return to **Grown-up Camp**, enter the Parent PIN if requested, and approve the mission.
7. Return to **Kid Quest Board**, open the approved reward, choose a **Spend**, **Save**, and **Share** plan, then tap **Feed the three jars**.

The Parent PIN is stored securely on the device. Parent access locks after five minutes, whenever the app enters the background, or when **Lock Grown-up Camp** is selected. Five incorrect attempts trigger a 60-second lockout.

### Account and iPad review path

Parent-gated account actions are under **Family Area** on the native home screen:

1. Open **Family Area > Account & Data**.
2. Complete the Parent Check or enter the Parent PIN.
3. Enter the review credentials supplied in App Review Information and tap **Sign In**.
4. Tap **Family Quest** in the account toolbar to return to the native Family Quest experience.

Build 13 fixes the iPad transition from the account experience to Family Quest. The app now waits for the account screen to finish dismissing before presenting Family Quest, preventing the second presentation from being dropped on iPad.

- Family Quest data remains on the device in this version.
- Rewards are virtual practice coins with no cash value.

- Embedded web navigation is restricted to Money Muncher first-party pages.
