For grown-ups / Updated November 13, 2026

Delete your account or data.

This page is for parents, guardians and teachers who have a Money Muncher (MoneyMuncher) account. You can delete the whole account, or ask us to remove part of the data saved with it. You do not need the app installed.

Delete my account Delete some of my data

Delete your account

Deleting a Money Muncher account is permanent. There are two ways to do it.

Delete it yourself, straight away

  1. Open the Money Muncher web account page in any browser and choose Log in.
  2. Sign in with the email address and password for the account.
  3. Choose Delete account in the top bar, then Delete permanently.

The account and its cloud-saved data are removed as soon as you confirm. On iPhone and iPad you can do the same in the app: open Family Area, choose Account & Data, sign in, then choose Delete account.

Open the account page →

Ask us to delete it for you

Use this if you cannot sign in or would rather not. Send the request form below and choose Delete my account and all its data. We reply to the account email address to confirm the request is yours, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: email address and password.
  • Your profile: the name or nickname, the role you chose (for example Family Team or Teacher Captain) and any beta-interest details saved with the account.
  • Game progress: coins, savings, joy and wisdom scores, levels, badges, completed lessons and the pretend-coin history.
  • Your Market Lab pretend portfolio.

What is kept, and for how long

DataKept for
Backup copies held by our cloud provider, Google Firebase. These expire on Google's schedule.Up to 180 days

Deletion requests and support messages you send us, kept as a record that the request was handled.Up to 12 months
Newsletter and workshop signups. These are separate from accounts. Remove them on the email preferences page.Until you ask

Before you delete. Removing the app does not delete an account. If you bought a subscription, deleting the account does not cancel it: cancel it first in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google, not by us.

Progress from guest play and the Chore Planner is saved only on your device. It never reaches our servers, and you can remove it by clearing the browser's site data or removing the app.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can ask us to remove part of what is saved without closing the account. Send the request form below and choose what to remove:

  • Game progress, so the account starts again from the beginning.
  • Your Market Lab pretend portfolio.
  • The name or nickname on your profile.

We reply to the account email address to confirm, then remove the data within 30 days. The same backup periods apply as for account deletion.

Request form

Reviewed by the MoneyMuncher team. Please do not send a password or a child's personal information. Privacy notice.

Questions

Children should ask a parent or guardian to make the request. For anything else about your account, use the support page.
