Money Muncher Parent Guide

Help & Support

How can we help?

Ask a question, report a problem, or request help with your Money Muncher account.

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send a password or a child's personal information.

Common questions

How do I permanently delete an account?

In the iOS app, open Family Area, choose Account & Data, sign in, choose Delete account, and confirm. The account and its cloud-saved profile and progress are permanently removed.

Cup Rush does not respond

Wait for the loading bar to finish, then use the on-screen arrow controls below the game. Keep the device connected to the internet while the game loads.

How is family data used?

Review the Money Muncher privacy notice for information about data handling.
