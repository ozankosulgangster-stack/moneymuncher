Workshop at a glance

Play. Talk. Try it in real life.

  • Ages: designed for kids 6–12
  • Length: approximately 60–75 minutes
  • Format: one caregiver participates with each family
  • Area: Greater Toronto Area venue to be confirmed
✓ Parent-present ✓ Play-based learning ✓ No child names collected online ✓ No financial-product sales

What to expect

A workshop built around family conversation—not lectures.

Every activity gives kids a choice, then gives families a simple way to talk about it without shame or pressure.

🏁

Family Money Quest

Work as a team through a familiar spending decision and discover the trade-offs together.

🏦

Spend, Save & Share

Give every pretend coin a job and connect the game to real family goals.

🛒

Needs vs. Wants Challenge

Make a playful plan under a budget and practise changing course when surprises appear.

💬

Founding-Family Feedback

Help shape future MoneyMuncher workshops and tell us what your family wants to learn next.

Why we’re doing this

Money confidence starts with small, safe conversations.

MoneyMuncher turns everyday choices into short family adventures. This workshop brings that experience off the screen so GTA families can learn from one another, test the activities, and help us build something genuinely useful.
