Parent Guide

Money Muncher is an arcade game that introduces money ideas through play. The first levels focus on collecting coins, avoiding debt, understanding that taxes reduce earnings, saving coins, and choosing simple gear upgrades.

What Kids Practice

  • Counting value through coin and treasure pickups.
  • Saving earned coins for gear.
  • Recognizing debt and tax as score-reducing events.
  • Making choices between speed and magnet upgrades.

Privacy-Safe Launch

The first public version is designed without accounts, chat, ads, analytics, or online leaderboards. Progress is stored locally in the browser.
