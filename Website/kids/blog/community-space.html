Building A Safer Community Space

Money Muncher can become more fun when players share ideas, but the kids section needs a careful first step. The new community hub focuses on weekly challenges, design prompts, and parent-approved submissions.

For now, there is no open comment box, no public chat, and no account requirement. That keeps the site simple while we design moderation, privacy, and review rules properly.

The next community layer can include featured challenge ideas, teacher prompts, and curated Money Park builds.
