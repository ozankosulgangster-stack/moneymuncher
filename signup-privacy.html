For grown-ups / Updated October 2, 2026

Email signup privacy.

These forms are for adult family members, educators and community contacts. Please do not submit children’s names, student information, real household finances or other sensitive details.

What the forms collect

The newsletter form collects your email address and consent. The workshop form also collects your adult contact name, preferred area and session format. We record which form you used and the consent version. Netlify hosts and processes the form submissions for MoneyMuncher; its service may retain submission metadata for spam protection and administration.

How we use it

Newsletter details are used for requested MoneyMuncher tips, resources and product updates. Workshop details are used for workshop planning and availability updates. The two lists are separate. Downloads do not require joining either list. Submissions are available to the site administrators and are not displayed publicly.

Change your mind

Use the email preferences form below to request removal from either list or deletion of a signup. Requests are reviewed by the MoneyMuncher team. This is a request form, not an instant account setting.
