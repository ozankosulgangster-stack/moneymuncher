Privacy notice.

This notice explains what Money Muncher (MoneyMuncher) collects, why, who receives it and what you can do about it. It covers moneymuncher.ca and the Money Muncher apps, which open pages from this site. Money Muncher teaches with pretend money only and never asks for bank or card details.

In short

  • The games can be played as a guest. Guest progress stays on the device.
  • An account is optional and is meant to be created by a parent, guardian or teacher.
  • Most pages on this site, including the kids pages and games, load Google Analytics and advertising tags from Google Ads and Reddit. These use cookies.
  • No adverts are shown inside Money Muncher, and there is no public chat, public profile or leaderboard.
  • You can delete an account or its data at any time.

What we collect

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account we collect an email address, a password, the name or nickname you enter, the role you choose (for example Parent Guide, Teacher Captain, Family Team or Kid Explorer) and any beta-interest details you add. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores the password in protected form; we cannot read it. We use the email address to verify the account, reset a password and reply to requests about the account.

Game progress

When you are signed in, progress is saved to the account so it follows you between devices: coins, savings, joy and wisdom scores, levels, badges, completed lessons, the pretend-coin history and the Market Lab pretend portfolio. Market Lab uses made-up prices and has no link to real trading.

Dino Munch chat

Messages typed into Dino Munch, and the address of the page the chat was opened on, are sent to our chat service, which runs on Microsoft Azure AI, to write a reply. Chat messages are not attached to an account. A reference to the conversation is kept on the device so the chat can continue, and the conversation is held in our Microsoft Azure service. Please do not type full names, addresses, school details, passwords or payment details into the chat.

Forms

The newsletter, workshop, support, email-preferences and deletion-request forms collect what you type into them, such as an email address, a contact name and your message. Form submissions are stored by Netlify, which hosts this site. The email signup notice has more detail on the newsletter and workshop lists.

Technical information

Like most websites, the services that deliver Money Muncher receive the IP address, browser and device type of each visitor in order to serve pages and keep the service secure. Some pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which also receives the IP address.

What stays on your device

  • On the web: guest progress, the role and learning path you picked, quests you generated and the Chore Planner.
  • In the app: family member names and avatars, family activities, goals and chores, and the grown-up PIN. Goal reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We learn only whether a subscription is active and never receive payment card details.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, real bank or card details, or a child's school.

Analytics and advertising tags

Most pages on this site load Google Tag Manager, which runs three services. This includes the kids pages, the games, the account page, Market Lab and the support page, and it applies when those pages are opened inside a Money Muncher app.

ServiceWhat it does
Google AnalyticsCounts visits and shows us which pages and features are used.
Google AdsMeasures whether our adverts lead to visits, and adds visitors to remarketing audiences so MoneyMuncher adverts can be shown to them on Google services.
Reddit Ads pixelMeasures whether our Reddit adverts lead to visits and lets Reddit show MoneyMuncher adverts to people who have visited.

These services set cookies (including _ga, _gcl_au and _rdt_uuid) and receive the IP address, browser and device details, the pages viewed and a cookie identifier. Our own code also tells them which campaign link brought a visitor and when a campaign quest is started or finished. Google and Reddit handle this information under their own privacy policies. Our code does not pass them names, email addresses, chat messages or game progress.

How to limit this. Block or clear cookies for moneymuncher.ca in your browser settings, switch off ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or change the advertising options in your Reddit account's privacy settings.

Children

Money Muncher is made for children to use with a parent, guardian or teacher. Children can play as guests without giving us a name or an email address. Accounts are meant to be created by an adult using the adult's own email address; the sign-up form does not ask for anyone's age.

The analytics and advertising tags described above also run on the kids pages and games.

If you believe a child has created an account or sent us personal information without your involvement, use the deletion page or the support page and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Delete an account or part of its data: see delete your account or data.
  • Leave an email list: use the email preferences page.
  • Limit analytics and advertising: see how to limit this above.
  • See or correct what we hold: ask through the support page.

Security

Pages and data are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Saved progress can be read and changed only by the signed-in owner of the account. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes and contact

When this notice changes we update the date at the top of the page. For any privacy question, contact the MoneyMuncher team through the support page.
