Accessibility

Money Muncher is a family financial-learning experience for iPhone and iPad. We aim to make its money missions, parent check, and learning stories clear and usable for as many families as possible.

Current Support

  • Native iOS controls are used for primary navigation, purchase actions, lesson controls, and parent checks.
  • Lesson controls include spoken narration and written lesson content.
  • We are evaluating common tasks with iOS and iPadOS accessibility features, including VoiceOver, Voice Control, and display preferences, before making App Store accessibility declarations.

Current Limits

  • Some character-led story screens include movement and are being evaluated with the system Reduce Motion setting.
  • Story narration is currently in English.
  • Written story content is available, but a full time-synchronized caption track is not yet available.

Our Ongoing Review

We test common tasks such as opening missions, using the parent check, restoring purchases, and completing learning lessons on both iPhone and iPad. We update the App Store accessibility information as features are verified.
