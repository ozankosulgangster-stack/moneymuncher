A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

Where should we start?

Try a free web quest together, download a teacher worksheet, or explore MoneyMuncher on iPhone and iPad. Choose the path that fits your family or classroom.

Are the teacher resources free?

Yes. The 14 Teacher Hub lessons, their worksheets and answer keys are free. Teacher Studio adds six extended lessons through the existing MoneyMuncher Plus subscription in the iOS app.

Are these official curriculum resources?

These are adaptable teaching activities, not a certified curriculum. Teachers can select and adapt the grade-band resources to their learners and local requirements.

Will joining the newsletter also sign me up for workshops?

No. Newsletter and workshop-interest lists have separate consent. Choose either, both, or neither. PDF downloads do not require signup.
