## Two-week promotion plan

### Week 1 — Founding-family launch

1. Publish the main announcement on Instagram and Facebook.
2. Share the four-frame Story sequence and save it as a “GTA Workshop” Highlight.
3. Post in 5–8 relevant GTA parent/community groups, respecting each group’s promotion rules.
4. Email existing parent subscribers.
5. Personally invite 10 trusted families and ask each to share with one other family.

### Week 2 — Trust and partnerships

1. Post a short video explaining why you are building the workshop.
2. Share one sample activity: “Give 10 pretend coins a Spend, Save, or Share job.”
3. Contact 10 potential hosts: libraries, community centres, schools, parent councils, newcomer organizations, and family resource centres.
4. Publish an FAQ post covering age range, parent participation, privacy, and the no-sales policy.
5. Share a progress update such as “Families from [areas] have joined”—only when supported by real registrations.

## Promotion guardrails

- Never publish photos or names of participating children without written caregiver consent.
- Clearly state that the workshop is free; do not imply that paid products or services are required.
- Do not imply guaranteed admission from the interest form.
- Avoid claims that the workshop guarantees financial success or changes behaviour.
- Keep the “educational only—not financial advice” and “no financial-product sales” language.
- When details are confirmed, replace all planning language with the exact date, venue, capacity, accessibility information, price, refund policy, and caregiver requirements.
